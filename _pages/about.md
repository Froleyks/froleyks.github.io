I am a computer scientist specializing in automated reasoning, SAT solving, and model checking. I am part of the core development team of the SAT solver [CaDiCal](https://github.com/arminbiere/cadical) and organize the [Hardware Model Checking Competition](https://hwmcc.github.io).

Since October 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher at KU Leuven with Prof. [Bart Bogaerts](https://www.bartbogaerts.eu/index.php). Before that, I completed my PhD with Prof. [Armin Biere](https://cca.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/biere/).

My research focuses on certification for PSPACE-hard problems. I aim to go beyond using certificates merely to prove correctness, to achieve provably correct verification and planning outcomes at scale.
